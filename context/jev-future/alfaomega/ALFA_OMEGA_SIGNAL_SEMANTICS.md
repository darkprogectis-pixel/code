## Rules

1. **No invented indicator.** Rows are discovered from real files: NT8 `Indicator`-derived classes and AOT BFF blocks, such as the `STATE = {…}` literal in `aot-bff.js`, and the knowledge corpora. Every row has `owner_file` and `owner_line`.
2. **Semantic fields only with evidence.** BUY, SELL and NEUTRAL meaning, signal role and unit come from curated entries whose `evidence {file, match}` resolves in the real file. If the evidence does not resolve, the build fails. Otherwise the field is `UNKNOWN` or `NOT_VERIFIED`. Currently 18 rows are VERIFIED and 111 are NOT_VERIFIED.
3. **DATA_SOURCE vs KNOWLEDGE_SOURCE.**
   - 124 rows have the data role DATA.
   - 5 rows are KNOWLEDGE: the SpotGamma course, the MenthorQ course and the Alpha video, skills and corpora. They never feed a value or a signal.
4. **Data sources found in code:** AO bridge :5151 (NT8), AOT BFF :3600, Consolidator / α Quant :3495, relay :3457 (GexBot, α Bot), QuantData :3490, MenthorQ :3480, SpotGamma :3500, plus INTERNAL calculations and NT8_NATIVE. No other API was added.
5. **Data states:** LIVE · DELAYED · STALE · MISSING · NOT_SUPPORTED · ERROR · UNKNOWN.
   - Missing ≠ 0.
   - UNKNOWN ≠ FAIL unless an explicit rule says so.
6. **Signal roles currently verified:**
   - JEV_FOUR_SOURCE_INPUT (4)
   - CONTEXT_NOT_SIGNAL (3)
   - NONE (6)
   - ALFABOT_GATE_INPUT (1: AoGate.EsNq)
   - ALFABOT_CONFIDENCE_INPUT (1)
   - All other rows are UNKNOWN. That is a known gap, not something to infer.
7. **Signal meaning:**
   - BUY or SELL is shown only when the ALFABOT engine published it.
   - NEUTRAL means no candidate.
   - WAIT means a candidate exists but a required condition fails or is unknown.
   - INVALID_DATA means transport or bridge is not live.
   - See `ALFABOT_SIGNAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
8. **MenthorQ** follows POSITIVE_CONFIRMATION_ONLY_NON_BLOCKING for JEV and for C11. The production strategy gate (C7) is an existing ALFABOT engine rule. It is explained, not changed.
